# 👿 BSD Community Gamejolt Rules 👿
## Rules:

Only post content directly connected to BSD (e.g., FreeBSD, OpenBSD, NetBSD, DragonFly BSD).

Do not mention Linux, Windows, or macOS, as they are not BSD operating systems, unless it is in direct reference to a comparison with BSD (e.g., BSD vs. Linux).

Please note that macOS and iOS are not permitted in this community. Although they share historical code bases like Mach and FreeBSD components via Darwin, they are proprietary, distinct operating systems, and do not fall under the BSD umbrella for this community.

You are entitled to your opinions as long as they are expressed respectfully.

Incorrect example: "THIS IS THE WORST OS EVER..."

For security reasons, it is strictly prohibited to post direct .iso or image file download URLs. You may only post links to the official main website where downloads are hosted.

PROHIBITION OF MALICIOUS, SUSPICIOUS, OR UNVERIFIED DISTRIBUTIONS & .ISO FILES.

## Rules for moderators:

BSD News posts must be directly relevant to the BSD ecosystem and must maintain a neutral, non-promotional tone (e.g., avoid titles like "THIS BSD DISTRO IS BETTER THAN LINUX"). Articles should provide a clear summary and include reliable sources where possible.

Fake or joke posts are strictly forbidden, with the sole exception of April Fool's Day for moderators. If you are a moderator, April Fool's pranks are only permitted in the official Announcement channel of this community.

Posting direct links from x.com (Twitter) is prohibited—even for sourcing purposes—unless xcancel.com is used. This is because X requires account registration to view content, which is no longer tolerated due to policies we deem unacceptable.
